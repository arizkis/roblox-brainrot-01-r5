Brainrot Bazaar is a 3D Roblox brainrot collection game. Buy characters off a conveyor, carry them back to your booth, and let them earn cash per second while you climb through rarities and mutations — then rebirth and start the climb again, richer.

It is built by an AI agent for the make.fun roblox-brainrot-01 bounty: a Rojo project whose Luau sources live in `src/`, built into `game.rbxl` and handed in by `main.ts`. The bounty brief is at https://make.fun/bounty/roblox-brainrot-01.

## How it plays

- **The loop**: brainrots ride the conveyor past the spawn arch — buy one, carry it over your head, drop it on a booth pad, and it earns cash every second.
- **Rarities**: 36 characters across 7 tiers, from a $25 Wet Floor Sign to the $150,000 Seraphim Server Rack; the spawn band shifts richer as the lobby rebirths.
- **Mutations**: Golden, Diamond, Glitch, Rainbow, Cosmic and Void rolls multiply income up to x15, each with its own visual treatment.
- **Tsunami**: every 90 s a wall of water sweeps the plaza from the river — get your cargo under a booth roof or lose it.
- **Steal & lock**: raid unlocked booths for their earners, or raise your own 60-second shield dome (90 s cooldown) to keep thieves out.
- **Raccoon thieves**: every ~75 s a raccoon slips through the fence and makes off with a character — whack it to drop the goods, or kiss them goodbye.
- **Rebirth**: trade 15x your income rate for a compounding x1.5 multiplier and an extra pad, keeping every character you placed.
- **Upgrades**: 6 tracks x 4 levels — speed, income, luck, carry speed, lock duration and pad polish.
- **Quests**: 3 rolling contracts from a pool of 12, paying cash on completion.
- **Index**: a 36-slot collection book that remembers every character you have ever owned.
